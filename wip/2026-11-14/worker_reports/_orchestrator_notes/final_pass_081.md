# One final pass: Kasandra drives the desk

## The gap today

- Her hand can only change data: files, pages, sheets, the Agenda.
- She cannot open the Field, select a sheet, open a right-click and choose a row, or turn the appearance.
- No Kernel operation drives the window. The only thing that drives it is a person's hand, or my offscreen probes, which reach into the code.

## The pass

A desk registry (`desk_ops`): one named act per thing a person can do in the window. Each act has an argument schema, a "navigates or changes" mark, and the function that does it. The first set, about 30 acts:

- **Go places:** open Home, the Field and its faces, Files at a folder, a page, a room, an app.
- **Point:** select a sheet, select a file, select a row in any tree.
- **The right-click:** open it on a thing, choose a row by its path (`more/compare:with`), carry the line panel (`step where unit=mA`, `count`, `show`).
- **The line:** put words in the typing line, or choose whom it goes to.
- **Look:** zoom the Field, show it whole, choose the appearance or the glow.
- **Read back:** what is open, what is selected, which rows a menu shows. The same words the window shows, as data.

Three doors, one registry:

1. **Her.** The model is offered the acts as tools. Navigating is hers at Do and show, because it changes nothing. A change still goes through her hand as it does now, with the card, the trail entry and the Undo, and what always asks still asks. Every act she takes is a line in her trail.
2. **You.** The same act typed in the line (`desk open field`), and a right-click row's tooltip can show the act that row is.
3. **Tests and the campaign.** My probes and the campaign's anchor situations become scripts of desk acts instead of reaching into widgets. A situation like "she opens the bench sheet, keeps the mA rows and counts them" becomes something the campaign can run, repeat and time, on the ThinkPad too.

It is also where the right-click's second half fits naturally. Save as a sheet, Watch it, Add to the Agenda and Keep as a word go through her hand with Undo, and her own acts and your clicks become the same thing.

## What it cascades into

- **Receipts** (idea 1): every desk act and every Kernel act leaves one record.
- **Simulation:** the campaign can act out your 35 situations with no person there.
- **The take-apart method** (idea 2): its steps can be shown to you by her, act by act.

## Size and order

- One pass: the registry, the 30 acts, the three doors, her hand's rules for navigation, tests for each act, and the campaign driving three anchor situations through it.
- The right-click's second half is the stretch goal, if time allows.
- Not in this pass: a model actually choosing acts on the ThinkPad. That needs the 35B loaded there, which is your step.

1. **Is navigation hers at Do and show** (open, select, zoom), or should even that ask on Ask first?
2. **Should a right-click row's tooltip name its act** (`desk.choose more/compare:with`), or keep that only in the line?
