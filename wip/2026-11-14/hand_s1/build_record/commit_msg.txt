Kasandra's hand, first slice: her door on the Field, with a planner over the desk

The owner: "We keep building for Kasandra's hand" and "Keep working on the big things you're smart about
like maths." S1 of the hand plan (judge_hand-plan.md, section 10): she is asked in Talk, in the owner's own
words; her hand finds the goals, plans the fewest acts from the window as read now, and makes them through
her own door, by pointer, reading the window back after each act. Navigation and reads only: every change
through her hand is refused in this slice.

- desk_ops.py (new, pure): the desk as a small state read back from read.desk, and the Field's acts as
  operators Op(name, pre, add, delete, certain, observe, cost): go.field, point.zoom whole, point.sheet,
  sheet.trace, sheet.compare, sheet.close, menu.close, read.desk (and go.back, for putting the window back
  only). Costs: the probes' seconds, then a running average.
- planner.py (new, pure): methods first (shown, traced, side_by_side, both_shown, tidy, put_back; a gap
  filled by a search of up to four acts), then a forward uniform-cost search; goals planned jointly, else
  one after another in the order said; budgets 4,096 expansions, 12 acts, 64 literals, TOO_FAR in words;
  ties on cost, length, names. Plans measure about 0.1 to 1.2 ms here.
- goals.py (new, pure): desk goals (seen and persist), words from templates only, evidence (the paths that
  matched and a digest of read.desk), the finish rule, and progress.
- hand_words.py (new, pure): the deterministic match (trace X / where X comes from, show or select X, X beside
  its other revision, close what is open; find_sheet's exact-then-unique rule), the GOAL turn's numbered goals
  (at most 16, and "none of these"), and the reading of a pick (1 to 3 different numbers in range, "none" alone).
- her_hand_qt.py (new): HerHand, one act per tick, at least 300 ms apart; her card in her column; the ring
  where her next press lands; Stop, Continue, Go ahead and Put my window back; the touch filter (a press
  whose receiver is a QWindow, not on her card; the wheel does not count); the stop flag; the snapshot and
  put back; session state her_plan; state_changed for her hologram (thinking and acting at work, waits waits
  for you, stopped, finished done). At Ask first the moves up to the next goal are offered and the Go ahead
  (or hand.approve) authorises exactly those, by fingerprint; a touch or a plan that changes them voids it.
- desk_doers: door 'her' (inside her turn only; owner-only acts, a by and every change refused; at Ask
  first OFFERED; the journal's OFFERED and STOPPED; origin stacy-hand:<turn>, correlation <turn>:<n>); the
  stop flag read before each act and between presses (key, type_text, walk, wait_until, the mouse); walk
  refuses a menu open on another target; read.desk says where the keyboard is, whether the line's words
  are an act's, an operation pending, the cards waiting, where the Field was entered from, the sheets in
  sight and the Field's sheets with their other revision; a trace only while its sheet is selected.
- field_qt: a trace gives way as soon as the selection moves (it waited for a painting with wires).
- desk_acts: hand.stop and hand.putback, the owner's own (42 acts, 35 exported); desk_hers refuses a by;

- stacy_hand: why_waits says "my hand is at Ask first" when the dial is the reason; the desk trail (200

- local_brain: brain.ask mode pick (her GOAL turn): no history, its own words, prompt at most 1,200
  tokens, answer at most 32, 60 s.
- shell2: SlateFace.plan_asked after run_asked; the Approve tooltips print no key. console_qt: the approval
