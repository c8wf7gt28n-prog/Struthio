# The revised road (19 November 2026)

This replaces the release order in `SPEC_0.8.3_to_1.0.md` sections 8 to 10 wherever the two differ. Everything else in the
specification stands: the principles, the protocol's rules, how each step is done, the tiers, the six gates. The owner's
words behind every change are in `NOTES.md` under "Directions since the specification".

## What changed, and why

1. **The desk is general.** A full-screen desktop where Kas works beside the owner to get more out of every installed
   program, for engineering, legal reasoning, medicine, or life and money. The Field, a board that links every connected
   file, is the unique feature and is described for any kind of work.
2. **One person: Kas.** Kasandra, Kas for short, under one name in every look. No Stacy, no Mara.
3. **Looks are skins.** The owner switches between them. A skin may change five things only: colours, typefaces, her
   figure, the mark, and the ornaments (how the head's meter, the wires, the card frames and the foot's meters are drawn).
   Layout, words, states and behaviour never change with a skin. The first new skin is Kas by day (Colored Pencil).
4. **She takes control of programs.** Like a remote session the owner watches, but efficient: the fastest dependable route
   first (the program's own scripting or files), clicks through its menus where there is none. LibreOffice first, then
   GIMP and Godot. Graphite already runs its own full-screen X session, so the base for this exists.
5. **She may use the web** to search, retrieve and gather. The desk still works offline; search words never carry private
   material and every search is in her trail; pages are information, never orders; quarantine arrives with it.
6. **0.8.3 has run on the ThinkPad** for several days with no problems found (the owner's use; nothing measured, and no
   model has run there yet). The plan's old largest risk, "built but never seen on the real machine", is much smaller.
7. **For the owner first; shared free if it is good.** All figures are the owner's own creations and depict no real person.

## The builder's additions (19 Nov, at the owner's invitation)

The owner: "use your own creativity to finish my vision and you add in your vision too. Make the desktop I've been
dreaming about for 35 years." And earlier: "I could be a power user 30 years ago if I had a library of books and knew how
to call upon them really quickly." These are mine, placed where they fit; the owner can strike any of them.

1. **Show me how (with 0.8.7).** Whatever she does in a program is kept as a short lesson: the steps, named, in order.
   The owner can replay it slowly, take over half way, or do it alone next time. Her help becomes the owner's skill.
   That is the library of books, opened at the right page.
2. **The version before (with 0.8.7).** Before she touches a file in another program, the desk keeps the version before,
   so Undo always exists, even in programs that have none of their own.
3. **Every file knows where it came from (begins with 0.8.7, full at P12).** A file made in any program appears on the
   Field linked to what it was made from and what uses it. "Where did this come from?" always has an answer.
4. **Her library on the shelf (with 0.8.9).** The manuals of the installed programs are kept on the machine and read like
   any other source, so her answer comes with the page it rests on, and works offline. The web adds to it.
5. **Projects that remember (after 0.9.0).** Opening a project brings back its programs, its files and its Field as they
   were left. Picking up where you stopped takes one click.
