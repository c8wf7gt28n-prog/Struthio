# X3 · Everyday sessions — the account

A walkthrough, not a test (the owner's rule: "No testing"). Each session was walked in Graphite's own window under Xvfb
(a real X server, Qt's xcb platform) with the stand-in window manager (S3's xworld), in the Day look at 1920x1200, on the
invented **Everyday Home** built through its own door (`python -m workbench.everyday_demo FOLDER`, a throwaway folder; the
window's HOME is that folder's `home`). The person's presses are the window's own: mouse clicks and double-clicks on the
tree and the board, the tree's right-click menus, the drag of a row onto a folder, the Machine page's buttons, her column's
ask line. Dialogs that would block (a name to type, a folder to pick) were answered with the words a person would type,
and every such dialog is listed. Stand-ins: udisks2/UPower/NetworkManager on a private session bus (S4's, adapted: the
HOLIDAY stick, plugged in on request, its photos appear on it when mounted), wpctl and xrandr (S4's), the web (K8's way),
logind (patched, nothing issued). Frames: `b100/X3/frames/` (`s<nn>_<k>_<what>_day_<W>x<H>.png`). Nothing here ran on
the owner's ThinkPad.

Her portrait shows in these frames (P4 removes it beside me; ignored here, as the coordinator said). Graphite runs as the
session here (ARC_LEDGER_XSESSION=1: its dock and its notification server stand); the stand-in manager reads no strut, so
the walk places Graphite's window below its dock, as Fluxbox would. The final frames: sessions 1 to 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12
at 1920x1200 with no model; 5, 6 and 13 at 1920x1200 with the real 4B ready; 1, 5, 6 at 1366x768 with no model.
