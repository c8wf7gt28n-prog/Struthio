# X4 · Power-user sessions: the account

A walkthrough, not a test (the owner's rule, 26 Nov: "No testing"). Each session was walked in the window, Graphite 1.0 at
the worktree's start commit (0.8.7 + every 1.0 slice through F1, F2 and R1) plus X4's own changes, on a throwaway copy, under
Xvfb with S3's stand-in window manager, S4's stand-in udisks2 / UPower / NetworkManager on a private session bus (grown here:
a second drive, a switch to drop the network, a busy unmount refused as the kernel refuses it) and stand-in `xrandr` and
`wpctl`, in the Day look. Nothing here ran on the owner's ThinkPad.

**The busy Home** (`workbench/power_demo.py`, built through its own door, `python -m workbench.power_demo FOLDER`, in about
15 s): X3 was not merged in this tree, so the busy Home is X4's own. `~/Downloads` holds 205 files (48 invoices, 23 bank
statements, 40 photos, 30 screenshots, 25 notes, 20 table exports, 10 archives and the copies a browser makes);
`~/Pictures/Camera` 24 photos of 16 pictures (the other 8 are the same bytes under later camera numbers; each carries
"Taken: …" in its own bytes); `~/Documents` a household budget (.ods and .csv), a bench table (.ods), a Rail Demo report
(.odt), a letter; two drives (a HOLIDAY stick and a BACKUP disk); and the workspace in `~/Concern/Ledger` with Rail Demo
built through its own door beside 240 pages, 40 ideas and an investigation of 30 findings and questions, all made through
`Kernel.dispatch` with the owner's principal: 284 objects on the desk by `field.read`, beyond 300 records with the ideas
(the Field does not count ideas). Everything is invented and says so.

How a step was taken: as a person reaches it wherever the window offers it (the foot's Line, Terminal, Search and Log, the
tree, the right-click entries, Waits for you, ACTIVE WORK's Stop, HALT, the board's tiles, Back and Forward, the Machine's
pages). Where no press exists for what a power user needs, or a step's set-up is what her plan would write, the walk called
the Kernel itself and says so ("the walk wrote …"). Stalls: every session ran with `GRAPHITE_STALL_LOG`; a stall while the
walk itself called the Kernel is counted apart from the window's own (the walk's own calls are not presses).

Frames are in `b100/X4/frames/` (`xNN_k_what_day_1920x1200.png`; 1366x768 for sessions 4, 5 and 10): 102 frames.

**The real 4B** (Qwen3.5-4B Q4_K_M, the bundled llama.cpp v1.0.0 b12359, CPU, under the model lock): the model check's tuning
sweep once (session 11, 410 s), and one window run with the 4B registered, activated and sealed through Graphite's own doors
(sessions 11 and 6: her utility draft and a question with HALT). Two earlier window runs meant for it did not load the model
(the walk's own job runner read the job's answer wrongly); they walked the no-model side of sessions 6 and 11.

## Session 1 · Lines and the Terminal

**What the person did.** Typed in Line, chaining four steps: `@files:Budget 2026/spending.csv | rows | where
Category=Groceries | where Date has 2026-11 | sum Amount`. Kept a line under a word (`word groceries = @files:Budget
2026/spending.csv | rows | where Category=Groceries | sum Amount`), ran the word, went on from it (`groceries | where Date
has 2026-10`), ran the same line on the other folder (`Budget 2025`), chained a grouping (`… | group Category | sum
Amount`). Switched the foot to Terminal and did the same sum with `awk` in the real shell, and `ls ~/Downloads | wc -l`.
Opened Log (Events) and filtered it by "groceries".

**What the window showed.** `x01_1_line_sum_november`: `302.62  12` (the exact sum of the twelve November groceries,
including 0.10 + 0.20 + 19.99 + 33.33; no rounding), "1 row · 0.28 s · read only · save as a sheet · watch it · add to the
Agenda · again". The word was kept ("Kept. "groceries" now runs: …"), and `groceries` ran as the whole line, written out
(`x01_3_word_ran`: 605.24 for both months, exact). The 2025 folder gave 603.24, exact (`x01_5_line_other_folder`).
Terminal (`x01_7_terminal_awk`): `~/Concern/Ledger/Files $ awk …` → `302.62`, "exit 0 · 0.0 s"; `ls ~/Downloads | wc -l` →
205. Log (`x01_9_log_events`) holds the shell commands ("Shell: · ls ~/Downloads | wc -l"), the line runs and the kept word.

**What went wrong or reads badly.**
- A kept word has no place for a folder: "run it again on another folder" means writing the whole line out again. A power
  user would want `groceries on "Budget 2025"` or a word with a slot. (coordinator: a design gap)
- `groceries | where Date has 2026-10` runs the step after the word's `sum`, so it answers "value · 0 rows" without saying
  why (the filter came after the total). A refusal in words ("this step comes after the total; put it before") would help.
- The grouping `… | group Category | sum Amount` was refused with "Graphite seed/source binding is not current" on the
  first walk: the seed did not match X4's changed files (a builder's copy, not the owner's case). With the seed rebuilt in
  the copy, as the merge does, it runs. Worth knowing: any line refuses this way while a seed is stale after an update.
- The foot's writing line shows the placeholder word `prompt` in every frame (an internal word on screen). (D2, words)
- Log's line rows read "Ran a line · failed" for a refused line, without the reason. (D2, words)
- The longest stall: 1341 ms at the first look at the tree (cabinet_qt … locations.marker); each line run 300-364 ms on the
  window's thread (typebar_qt.submit_line … stacy_foundation).
