DEMO TOOLS (INVENTED) · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: How do I reset the bench meter to factory settings?
A: Hold the RANGE button while switching the meter on, then release it after the beep.
Q: Does the meter come with a case?
A: Yes, a soft case and a pair of test leads.
Battery: one 9 V battery, about 200 hours.
Q: Which fuse does the meter use?
A: A 10 A ceramic fuse, 6.3 x 32 mm.
