HOME DEMO · TENANCY AGREEMENT · RIDER C (INVENTED)
INVENTED: NOT THE LAW OF ANY PLACE · 22 Sample Road, Demotown

C1. Notice. The tenant may end the tenancy by giving 2 months' notice in writing.
C2. Garden. The tenant keeps the front garden tidy; the landlord cuts the hedge twice a year.
C3. Inspections. The landlord gives one week's notice before an inspection.
C4. Keys. The tenant receives two sets of keys and returns both at the end.
Lost keys are replaced at the tenant's cost.
