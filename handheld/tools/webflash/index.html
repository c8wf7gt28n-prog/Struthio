STRUTHIO

Put the game on your Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5B board, straight from this page. Nothing to install.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (phones and Safari cannot reach USB).
  2. Plug the board in with a USB-C data cable.
  3. Press Install STRUTHIO, pick the port that appears (often "USB JTAG/serial debug unit"), confirm.
  4. Wait about two minutes. When it says it is done, unplug the board: the first power-on check starts.
This browser cannot reach USB. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer, or use FLASH_ME.bat from the package. This page must be opened over https (or from localhost) to reach USB.

No port appears? Put the board in download mode: hold BOOT, press and release RESET, release BOOT, then press Install again. In a finished case the BOOT and RESET keys are the two small pin holes on the left side.

It writes five files: the bootloader, the partition table, the game, its pictures (0x410000) and its music (0xD10000), and checks each one.

The flasher could not start. Use FLASH_ME.bat from the package instead.
