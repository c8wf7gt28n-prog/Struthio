hatch_egg_{64,128,256,512}.png (UI-03, the owner's request, 2026-11-05)
  The owner's egg, from the attached illustration: the spotted cracked egg with two mushrooms and sparks, transparent
  background, trimmed (the 128 is 128 by 116). It is the Hatch tile at the left of the typing strip, drawn unnecessarily
  large: 44 px high at the default 14 pt interface size, scaled with the interface size up to 1.4 (typebar_qt.HATCH_ICON,
  hatch_icon_px). hatch_qt.HatchButton uses the 128 at device pixel ratio 1 and the 256 above it; the 64 and 512 are kept
  for other sizes. Dominant colours, for checks: cream #f0e0c0, orange-red #f04000, black outlines.

hatch-{16,24,32,48}-{normal,hover,pressed,disabled}.svg
  The earlier drawn faces (original SVG artwork): the spotted cracked egg alone at 16, with two simple mushrooms above.
  Paper palette: warm paper, graphite, pencil yellow and eraser pink, matching the Struthio design mark. Since UI-03 the
  tile shows the egg PNG; these faces are the fallback when the PNG is missing from the program folder, and they remain
  for any other place that wants a small Hatch mark.
