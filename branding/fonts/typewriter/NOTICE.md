# Display font (the Kasandra appearance)

Special Elite is bundled unmodified under the Apache License, version 2.0 (SpecialElite-LICENSE.txt). Font name and
copyright belong to their owners (Astigmatic One Eye Typographic Institute). It is the worn typewriter face of headings in
the Kasandra appearance only: the greeting, the Field's heading, the workspace title. Values, receipts, cells and controls
stay in IBM Plex.

Source: the owner's Kasandra pack (Pass 02, fonts/), a copy of the Google Fonts release.
