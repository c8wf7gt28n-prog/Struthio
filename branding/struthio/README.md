# The Struthio

The owner's own picture of a Struthio, an ostrich drawn with circuit traces (20 November 2026). It is the Apps button in the
head's upper right, in both looks, "like a windows start menu" (0.8.6; `workbench/struthio_mark.py`). The button keeps its
name Apps for readers and its tip. The window's own icon is unchanged, and the neon kp logo stays in the program
(`branding/kasandra/kp_neon.png`).

- `struthio.png`: the drawing on no ground, 373 x 512, cut by `tools/cut_struthio.py` from the owner's file: the white
  ground joined to the picture's edge made clear, each rim pixel keeping the drawing's colour with the share it holds as
  its alpha (no white fringe on a dark band), trimmed to the drawing (x 102-973, y 104-1299 of the file) and drawn down
  smoothly to 512 px tall. It is shown only when its bytes are these:
  SHA-256 217902d73e19e5f730faf882dc670b5fbf3a99fe04e3ec2dbad7ddcfc85de45b.
  The owner's file (1120 x 1404, on white): SHA-256 22ad9345e52622dcbd61a67c209ef5a51446fe49da827e3394508007f2b92e42
  (not kept here).

It is the owner's art, like his other pictures under `branding/`: kept back by the owner (RIGHTS.md). It may be copied
unchanged together with Graphite and for no other use.
