# PCB layer — R22

**Status: order files ready. One part to source: JLCPCB had no stock of the ESP32-P4NRW32X (U1) on 2026-10-07 (see *Sourcing U1*).**

Editable source: `SLIM4_R22.kicad_pcb`. Open `SLIM4_R22.kicad_pro` in KiCad 7.0.x with this folder as the project directory; `fp-lib-table` points at the local `SLIM4.pretty` library. `SLIM4_R22_PCB_LAYER.json` is the read-only interchange projection the case checks and the studio read; `CHECKS/export_pcb_layer.py` writes it from the board.

R22 is R21 plus the fixes from the electrical and land-pattern review in `ELECTRICAL_REVIEW_R22.md`. The R21 board is kept unchanged in `REFERENCES/PCB_R21/`.

## What changed from R21

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | USB-C D+/D− now go to GPIO25/GPIO24 (USB-Serial-JTAG) instead of the USB 2.0 high-speed PHY pins 50/49. The existing inner-layer pair is kept; D− reaches pad 52 inside the pad ring, and D+ drops through one new via to pad 53. Two front-side tracks (FB_DCDC, CHIP_PU) are jogged around the via, and a redundant In3 GND tie is removed. Nets renamed `USB_JTAG_DP/DM`. | One USB-C cable then gives flashing with auto-reset (`idf.py flash`), the ROM boot log, the app console and JTAG debugging, all in ROM with no firmware support needed. On the high-speed port, auto-download stops working as soon as an app uses USB, and the console and JTAG need extra work. |
| 2 | U11, U12 (TPD2EUSB30): KiCad `Texas_DRT-3` footprint; local D+/D− and CC1/CC2 routes redrawn across the pads, GND to a via. | The R21 proxy was SOT-23 sized (pads 1.9 mm apart). The part is the 1 × 1 mm DRT package and could not have been soldered. |
| 3 | J3, J4, J5 (JST SH): KiCad `JST_SH_SM0xB-SRSS-TB` footprints with both solder tabs. J4 moved 2.7 mm inboard. | The proxies had no tab pads, so the connectors would have been held only by their signal pins. With the real body depth, J4's tabs fell off the board edge. |
| 4 | D1 (PESD5V0S1UL, SOD-882), Y1 (Lucki 3225 crystal), SW5–SW7 (Omron B3U-1000P): KiCad library land patterns; nearby GND tracks moved clear of the larger pads. | The proxies' pads did not match the parts' terminals (D1 pads 0.2 mm off each end; B3U pads short of the terminals). |
| 5 | C203, C204: 18 pF → 12 pF C0G (0402CG120J500NT, LCSC C1547, JLC Basic). | Y1's load capacitance is 10 pF: 12 pF in series pairs to 6 pF, plus about 4 pF of pin and trace stray. |
| 6 | C603 moved 0.15 mm. | Courtyard clear of J3. |
| 7 | R309 24.9 Ω → 5.1 Ω (0603WAF510KT5E, C25197); C309 1 µF 25 V → 1 µF 50 V (CL10A105KB8NNNC, C15849). | Backlight set to 39 mA for the chosen panel's two 7-LED strings (40 mA rated). The boost's open-LED limit is 38 V, so the output capacitor must survive it when no panel is plugged in. |
| 8 | U6 TLV75528 → 3.0 V LDO (TLV75530; ME6211C30 for stock, row 10); net LCD_2V8 → LCD_VCI_3V0. | Panel analog supply 3.0 V: mid-range for ST7703 / ILI9881C / ST7701S panels, closer to the panel's 3.3 V rating. |
| 9 | J3 battery socket: JST SH 3-pin → 2-pin (the speakers' part, SM02B-SRSS-TB, C160402): pin 1 BAT+, pin 2 GND. New R424 10 kΩ (C25744) from the charger's TS pin to GND; the NTC route is cut back to it (net BAT_NTC → BQ_TS). | Any 2-wire cell plugs in (on a JST SH 1.0 2-pin plug), instead of only a cell with a 10 k thermistor lead. The BQ24074 datasheet specifies 10 k to ground when no thermistor is used. Over-charge, over-discharge and short-circuit protection stay on the cell's own protection board; charging no longer pauses for cell temperature. |
| 10 | JLCPCB stock substitutions, same package and value: 10 µF 0805 → CL21A106KAYNNNE (C15850); 22 µF 0805 → CL21A226MQQNNNE (C5674); 4.7 µF 0603 → CL10A475KO8NNNC (C19666); 499 k → 0402WGF4993TCE (C25793); 4.02 k → 0402WGF4021TCE (C43251); U6 → ME6211C30M5G-N (C403651, same SOT-23-5 pinout IN/GND/EN/NC/OUT, 3.0 V); SW1–SW4 → Omron D2LS-21 (C3823622). | Those parts had no stock at JLCPCB (checked 2026-10-07). D2LS-21 has the D2LS-11's free/operating positions (3.5 / 3.2 mm) and land pattern; its operating force is 0.6 N instead of 1.2 N, the same as the flap switches already used. |
| 11 | L1 (MWSA0402S): pads 1.5 × 2.5 mm at ±1.85; L2 (ASWPA4035), L3 (WPN4020): 1.1 × 3.7 mm at ±1.5; one 3V3_SYS track under L2 moved 0.9 mm. Footprint descriptions of the other audited proxy lands updated. | Sunlord's recommended lands. The proxies stopped 0.35 mm short of L1's terminal toe and were narrower than L2/L3's terminals, leaving no side fillet. |


Board outline, stackup, thickness, every other part and the J1/J2 and switch positions are unchanged from R21. The case checks against R22 are in `CHECKS/R27_CONVERGENCE_REPORT.md`; J5's real JST body now meets a rear support post, a case item for the case pass.

## Sourcing U1 (ESP32-P4NRW32X)

On 2026-10-07 every other BOM line is in stock at JLCPCB. U1 shows 0 at JLCPCB and LCSC under all ESP32-P4 part numbers, and DigiKey/Mouser list no stock for the bare chip. Ways through, in order of preference:
1. **JLCPCB Global Sourcing / parts pre-order** (Parts Manager → order the part for your library): JLCPCB buys it and holds it for your PCBA order. Lead time is set by their supplier.
2. **Consign the chips**: buy ESP32-P4NRW32X (revision v3.x; the ordering code ends in X) from an Espressif-authorised source and send them to JLCPCB's parts library. Avoid brokers without traceability: counterfeit or v1.x chips would not boot with this board's v3 power network.
3. Order now with U1 marked "do not place", and have U1 fitted later by a rework shop. It is a 0.35 mm-pitch QFN-104, so hot-air rework needs a shop, not a hobby iron.

Do not substitute ESP32-P4NRW32 (no X, chip revision v1.x): its core-supply feedback network and pin 54 differ.


## Rebuild

`R22_FROM_R21/build_r22.sh <copy of REFERENCES/PCB_R21/SLIM4_R21.kicad_pcb, beside its .kicad_pro and SLIM4.pretty>` regenerates R22 from the unchanged R21 board, one scripted edit at a time, then refills zones and runs DRC. The result is copper-identical to `SLIM4_R22.kicad_pcb`. It needs KiCad 7.0.x with its Python module and the `kicad-footprints` 7.0.x library in `/usr/share/kicad/footprints`. `R22_FROM_R21/fpcmp.py <board> REF=PACKAGE ...` compares board footprints with the KiCad library footprint for their package.
