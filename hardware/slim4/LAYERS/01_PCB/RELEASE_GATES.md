## Open

1. **U1 stock.** ESP32-P4NRW32X had no stock at JLCPCB, LCSC, DigiKey or Mouser on 2026-10-07. See *Sourcing U1* in `README_PCB_LAYER.md`.
2. **Rotations in JLCPCB's placement preview.** Check pin 1 of every IC, diode, connector and the crystal before confirming assembly. J1 is a top-contact socket on the front, mouth toward the board's bottom edge.
3. **Panel support.** The panel sits over the board's front on its folded tail and overhangs the top edge by 13.6 mm; it needs 2.3 mm over J1 and about 3.3 mm at the fold. Until the case pass, mount it on 3–4 mm foam spacers.
4. **Bring-up on the first boards** (no case needed), phase by phase as `BRINGUP_PROCEDURE.md` lists it: rails (SYS_RAW, 3V3_SYS, 1V1_HP, LCD 1.8 / 3.0 V, backlight boost) on a current-limited supply, with the switch-node waveforms, output ripple and regulator temperatures of U4, U3 and U7 at full load (R24's and R25's layout is checked by DRC, not measured), a stable image on the coupled pairs at 560 Mbit/s a lane and then at 1000 Mbit/s, and the 1V1_HP rail at U1 under load (1.20–1.28 V, never above 1.30 V; the regulator is 12–21 mm away, R26 feeds the south-row core pins' via through a 0.4 mm In2 channel, and R27's exposed-pad vias take 8 % of that In2 area; C138/C139 are the local bulk); USB-Serial-JTAG enumerates and `idf.py flash monitor` works; flash and PSRAM tests; charger with a cell (and a reversed cell: the board must stay off); audio on both amplifiers; panel init and backlight (`firmware/slim4/docs/FIRST_BOOT.md`).
5. **U15 sourcing.** The RAK3172-SIP-9-SM-NI has no LCSC listing: JLCPCB Global Sourcing by part number, or consign it. It is MSL 3 (bake 12 h at 125 °C past its floor life).
6. **Radio on the bench** (`BRINGUP_PROCEDURE.md`, rows B8–B9, C14, D13 and phase I): the AT link, a ping between two boards, TX current and supply droop, and range to 200 yd and beyond. The RF match is RAKwireless's (0 Ω, shunts empty); a tuned match needs a VNA and is not planned.
7. **Antenna.** Any 915 MHz antenna on a U.FL plug plugs into J701. Its type and place in the enclosure are part of the case pass, as is a new place for the R12 rear support post at (−21.5, 76.0), which now lands on L701 (convergence check I1; the post also meets C718, C720 and L701 in check F1).
8. **Radio firmware recovery.** The SiP ships with RUI3 and R702 keeps it starting RUI3. If its firmware were ever lost, a wire from R702's BOOT0 pad to R703's RADIO_3V3 pad starts its ROM bootloader on U1's UART, but writing RUI3 back needs a UART pass-through in U1's firmware, which is not written yet.
9. **Regulatory.** A radio built from a SiP is not covered by a module grant: these boards are prototypes for the owner's own use. A product sold with this radio needs its own FCC (and, outside the US, other) authorisation.
