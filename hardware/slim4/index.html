STRUTHIOHARDWARE VISUALIZATION STUDIO · 1.2

Primary in-house file-authoritative hardware visualization platform for the STRUTHIO Project.

Created in necessity for R.A. Peddycoart to visualize, inspect, discuss, and iterate STRUTHIO hardware with AI collaborators.

LOCAL-FIRSTKiCad and CALC-3D files are parsed on-device by the studio.
ENGINEERING VIEWInspect the three physical parent layers — Case, PCB, Acrylic — and scrub the complete front-to-back build stack.
PORTRAIT INSTRUMENTOne-finger orbit, two-finger translation, pinch zoom, movable inspector, and scroll-wheel controls.
BENCHSYSTEMS colours the board by function; BENCH records each board's bring-up against the procedure; DOCS searches the package documents offline. Records stay in this browser until exported.
AUTHORITY RULELoaded fabrication sources are authoritative. Missing sources remain visibly unresolved; the studio does not invent replacement geometry.
