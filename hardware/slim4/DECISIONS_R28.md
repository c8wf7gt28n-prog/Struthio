# R28 decisions

R28 replaces PCB R22 with PCB R23, an all-in-one main board with four plug-and-play ports. The owner asked for the panel cable to go into the main board ("forget the case, design the ultimate PCB, fully ready for four plug-and-play connections, with firmware"). Each decision names the file that carries it. `DECISIONS_R27.md` and `DECISIONS_R26.md` still hold for everything not listed here.

| # | Decision | Why | Supersedes | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Display: Crystalfontz CFAF7201280A0-050TN**, 5.0 in 720 × 1280 IPS, ILI9881C, 2-lane DSI at 1 Gbit/s. | Public datasheet and pin table, init sequence in mainline Linux, Espressif `esp_lcd_ili9881c` driver, single units sold. Its 40 mm tail reaches the main board on its own. | R27 #3 (Startek KD047HDFID001, pin table not public) | `LAYERS/01_PCB/DISPLAY_PORT.md` |
| 2 | **No adapter cable: the panel's tail plugs straight into J1**, a Hirose FH12A-40S-0.5SH(55) top-contact socket on the board's front at (1.9, 76.0), pad k = panel pin k. | The datasheet folds the tail once behind the module; folded, its contacts face away from the board, so the socket is top contact, on the front, 34.3 mm above the panel's bottom edge. One less part and one less connector in the DSI path. | R27 #4 (display flex R1, now `REFERENCES/DISPLAY_FLEX_R1/`) | R23 edit 15; check N1 |
| 3 | **Battery window 2.5 mm shorter** (38 × 53.5 mm): cells up to 34 × 50 × 7 mm (503450 / 703450). | J1 has to sit 34.3 mm above the panel's bottom edge, which lands it just below the old window edge. | the 34 × 52 mm bay of R25 | R23 edit 15; `LAYERS/02_CASE/build_r12.py` |
| 4 | **Battery: JST PH 2.0 socket (J3) behind a reverse-polarity switch** (Q2 AO3401A). Charge current 494 mA (R412 1.8 k). | The plug hobby 1-cell packs use. | 5 | **Speakers: Molex PicoBlade sockets (J4, J5)**, pin 1 +. | The plug small speakers ship with (Adafruit 3923 / 4227). | JST SH 2-pin with leads soldered on | R23 edit 13 |
| 6 | **Backlight 74 mA** (R309 2.7 Ω): 37 mA in each of the panel's two strings. | Full brightness within the 40 mA string rating and the boost's 84 mA worst case. | R22's 39 mA | R23 edit 14 |
| 7 | **DSI routed for 1 Gbit/s**: P = N within 0.01 mm, flight-time skew ≤ 8 ps in a pair and ≤ 39 ps clock to data; standard JLCPCB stackup, no impedance control (outer pairs about 90–108 Ω). | Under 0.04 UI of the ~0.2 UI D-PHY leaves the channel; within 100 Ω ± 10 %. | R22's "impedance control not needed at 29–40 mm" | `LAYERS/01_PCB/README_PCB_LAYER.md` |
