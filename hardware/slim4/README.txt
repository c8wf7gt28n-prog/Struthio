STRUTHIO SLIM4 — R32 PACKAGE

R32 puts PCB R27 in the package: PCB R26 with the margins found by a five-part pre-order review
(CHECKS/PREORDER_REVIEW_R26/: power, processor, display, audio and controls, firmware; no blocker in
the hardware). 31 ground vias in U1's exposed pad (R26: 4), each on the pad's copper; 10 uF at
U1's core supply pins; a second 1 uF on the backlight output and a 60 V backlight diode; R424
beside the charger (BQ_TS 5.2 mm, was 88 mm); 22 ohm on the USB data lines; silkscreen labels at
the battery, speaker and button positions. The ground under the core regulator's feedback and
enable routes is checked unbroken (LAYERS/01_PCB/R27_FROM_R26/PLANE_CHECK_R27.txt). The DSI pairs,
pins, ports and outline are unchanged. Four parts plug into it with no soldering and no adapter
cables: the Crystalfontz 5 in panel (its own tail into J1), a protected 1-cell battery of 1000 mAh
or more (JST PH, reverse-polarity switch), and two speakers (Molex PicoBlade). The firmware for it
is firmware/slim4 in the repository (R12: a hardware self-test at every boot and a USB console for
bring-up, docs/FIRST_BOOT.md). LAYERS/01_PCB/BRINGUP_PROCEDURE.md is the probe procedure for the
first boards. What R32 decided and why is in DECISIONS_R32.md (R31's to R26's decisions still hold
where not replaced). The case was set aside by the owner.

  01_PCB          R27: the four plug-in ports, a top-contact display socket on the front under the

  03_ACRYLIC      R2: clear unprinted face film (on hold with the case).

CHECKS/convergence_check.py tests every interface between the layers:
  - board against the walls, LCD against the shell, caps against the switches, film against the relief;
  - parts against parts, at rest and pressed;
  - supports against pads, joints, speakers, USB-C and battery;
  - J1's 40 pads against the panel's published pin table (N1).
Result: 78 PASS · 3 FAIL · 9 GATE · 3 INFO (CHECKS/R32_CONVERGENCE_REPORT.md).
The 3 FAIL rows are the set-aside case: the 5 in panel does not fit the R12 pocket (C6), and the
rear shell meets the new J3/J5 bodies and sits 0.08 mm from J4 (F1, G1). PRODUCTION_GATES.md lists
what is open, in the order to do it.

Shared CAD datum: millimetres, SLIM4 PCB XY (R21 = R22 = R23 = R24 = R25 = R26 = R27; Y down), board bottom at Z=0, +Z
toward the front.

Regenerate everything (Python 3.13 with requirements.txt; KiCad 7.0.x for the PCB steps, with its
pcbnew module importable from that Python). Run from
the package root, in this order:
  python3 CHECKS/export_pcb_layer.py LAYERS/01_PCB/SLIM4_R27.kicad_pcb REFERENCES/PCB_R21/SLIM4_R21_PCB_LAYER.json \
      LAYERS/01_PCB/SLIM4_R27_PCB_LAYER.json R27 "STRUTHIO SLIM4 PCB R27" "Order files ready · U1 stock to confirm"
                                         (only after a PCB edit; then update the A1 hashes)
  python3 CHECKS/dsi_pair_check.py LAYERS/01_PCB/SLIM4_R27.kicad_pcb CHECKS/R27_DSI_REPORT   (after a PCB edit)
  python -B LAYERS/02_CASE/export_layer_formats.py
  python -B CHECKS/build_builder_packs.py <folder outside the package>
                                         (writes CHECKS/R27_FAB_SUMMARY.json)
  python -B CHECKS/convergence_check.py
  python -B CHECKS/build_builder_packs.py <folder outside the package>   (again: its README quotes the report)
  python -B CHECKS/render_review.py
  python -B CHECKS/build_pcb_viewer_data.py
