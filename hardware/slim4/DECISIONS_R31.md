# R31 decisions

R31 replaces PCB R25 with PCB R26 after the R30/R25 pre-order audit, which held the PCB order for one DSI routing revision: the six DSI lines were flight-time matched but routed as single lines, not coupled pairs. The audit also found the stackup's centre prepreg out of date, the 1V1_HP rail described as 1.1 V, and the firmware's pin check dependent on files outside the firmware folder. The owner agreed the plan and handles the CPU sourcing (ESP32-P4NRW32X, v3.1 preferred); battery and case stay on hold. Each decision names the file that carries it. `DECISIONS_R30.md` to `DECISIONS_R26.md` still hold for everything not listed here.

| # | Decision | Why | Supersedes | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Route the three DSI pairs as coupled pairs**: constant width and gap per layer, P and N on the same layers through the same vias, from U1's pads to J1's. | A D-PHY lane is a differential pair; the receiver sees the difference, and an uncoupled pair picks up and radiates common-mode noise. R25's lines changed layer 2–7 times each, P and N differently. | R30 #3 (match in flight time, lines routed singly) | R26 edits 31–32, `R26_FROM_R25/` |
| 2 | **100 Ω differential: F.Cu 0.127 mm with a 0.18 mm gap, In3 0.10 mm with 0.18 mm**, impedance control ordered for the pairs. | Hammerstad-Jensen with the coupled-microstrip factor gives about 100 Ω on F.Cu over In1 (0.0994 mm 3313); IPC-2141 asymmetric stripline with the coupled factor about 98 Ω on In3 (0.1088 mm to In2, 0.35 mm to In4). The gap is the same on both layers, so tapers are only needed at vias and pads. | R30 #5 (50 Ω single-ended widths) | order README, `README_PCB_LAYER.md` |
| 3 | **Fix each pair's side of U1**: CLK straight up over U1's west half on F.Cu; D0 and D1 east under U1 on In3 and up the east column on F.Cu, D1 inside D0. | U1 orders its pins D0, CLK, D1 and J1 orders them CLK, D1, D0, so one pair must pass the others; pairs cannot cross on one layer, and a negotiated router left to itself keeps choosing crossings. This is the one arrangement that converged with every clearance met. | — | `dsi_pair_router.py` (`pairs_east`) |
| 4 | **Match lengths, then compensate skew inside each pair**: coupled serpentines bring every pair to the longest (0.201 mm spread, limit 0.762), then 45° teeth on the shorter line remove what the pair's turns leave (at most 0.031 mm, limit 0.254). | Within a coupled pair the lengths, not a per-layer timing model, are what the audit's limits and Espressif's guideline state; both lines of a pair share a layer at every point, so equal lengths are equal delays. | R30 #3 | `dsi_geometry.py`, `CHECKS/R26_DSI_REPORT.md` |
| 5 | **Move EN_DCDC and four slow nets** (USB_CURR_OUT2, BTN_LEFT, PGOOD_STATUS, PWR_WAKE) and nudge CHG_STATUS and the backlight pair. | They held the only routes: EN_DCDC's F.Cu column split the free area over U1 in two (CLK's matching needs it), the others crossed the escape under U1 and the entry to J1. All are static levels or a button. | — | R26 edits 31 and 34 |
| 6 | **Ground-plane antipads 0.15 mm** (was 0.2) on In1, In4 and the In2 DSI band; the pairs keep the board's 0.12 mm from via pads. | A pair edge passing another net's via sat up to 0.08 mm over that via's antipad. Holding the pairs 0.15 mm off every via pad closed D0's only In3 lane under U1 (0.02–0.04 mm short); smaller antipads (the Default net class's own clearance) cut the overlap to at most 0.040 mm in 13 places, with no DSI track over any gap or split. | — | R26 edits 31, 33 |
| 7 | **Return vias by reference change**: a ground via each side, or one on the pair's axis, where a pair moves between differently referenced layers (B.Cu or In3 to F.Cu); none where both layers share In4 (B.Cu to In3). | The return current must cross between planes only where the reference plane changes. Mirror-symmetric placement where space allows; on the axis (equal distance to P and N) for CLK, whose sides are taken by D1's breakout. | R30's "a ground via beside every DSI via" | R26 edits 32–33 |
| 8 | **Keep U1's south-row 1V1 feed on the plane through a 0.4 mm In2 channel.** | The new In2 band under D0 would have cut that 1V1 via off. The channel stays north of D0 (0.32 mm of band between them along it, 0.22 mm at the via). | — | R26 edit 33 |
| 9 | **Stackup 2116 prepreg 0.1088 mm**; **1V1_HP called 1.2 V** wherever it was called 1.1 V (net name kept). | JLCPCB's calculator lists 0.1088 mm today; the TLV62569's 499 k / 499 k divider on its 0.6 V reference sets 1.2 V. | R25 metadata; earlier wording | `r26_meta.py`, docs |
| 10 | **Firmware R10: the pin check carries its own U1 pad table** (`tools/u1_pad_nets.json`), checked against the board export inside the repository. No firmware source changes. | The firmware zip could not run its own check. | R9's `check_pinmap.py` | `firmware/slim4` |
| 11 | **Route offline, draw from data**: the router and geometry tools write `r26_dsi_routes.json` and `r26_nets.json`; the build draws them. | The board rebuilds the same without the search, and the routes can be reviewed as data. | — | `R26_FROM_R25/` |
