# R32 decisions

R32 replaces PCB R26 with PCB R27 after the pre-order review of R26 and firmware R11: five independent reviews, each checking every pin of its subsystem against the datasheets (`CHECKS/PREORDER_REVIEW_R26/`). They found no blocker in the hardware, three firmware faults that would have kept every screen dark, and a list of layout margins. The owner chose "margin fixes + extras" in one more board revision before ordering, checked with DRC and the existing checks. The owner handles the CPU sourcing; battery and case stay on hold. `DECISIONS_R31.md` to `DECISIONS_R26.md` still hold for everything not listed here.

| # | Decision | Why | Supersedes | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ground vias in U1's exposed pad: 4 → 31**, every one joined to the pad's copper: 15 wholly inside its nine 2.1 mm squares, 16 at their edges or in the gaps, joined to a square on B.Cu (the new ones by a 0.25 mm stub). | The pad is the ESP32-P4's only ground; Espressif asks for as many vias as fit. A via in a gap without a stub would reach the inner planes only (the gaps are under mask), so it would carry no current or heat from the pad. | R26's 4 vias | R27 edit 36, `r27_u1_ground.py` |
| 2 | **10 µF bulk at the core pins** (C138 north, C139 south), joined to the existing 1V1_HP copper; C139 with its own via into the In2 1V1 plane. | U1's four VDD_HP pins had 100 nF each and the only bulk (C135, 22 µF) sat 13–16 mm away at L2; Espressif's reference has 10 µF at VDD_HP_0. C138 is 1.9 mm from pin 91; C139 3.7 mm from the south-row pins (the review asked for 3 mm; nothing closer was free). | — | edit 37 |
| 3 | **Keep the FB_DCDC / EN_DCDC routes; check the ground under them instead.** | The divider is within 3 mm of U3's FB pin, so the long trace carries the regulated output, not the high-impedance node; its same-layer neighbours are static nets. Moving U3 to U1 would move a dozen parts and the DSI escape, a far larger change than the risk it removes. `r27_plane_check.py` proves unbroken ground under all 58.8 mm and names the 5 small crossings. | the option text "shorter, shielded" | `r27_plane_check.py`, `PLANE_CHECK_R27.txt` |
| 4 | **C140 beside C309; D2 60 V.** | At 24 V a 1 µF 50 V X5R 0603 keeps roughly half its value, below the TPS61165's minimum; D2's 40 V left 1–3 V over the open-LED limit. Same footprint for D2. | — | edits 39–40 |
| 5 | **R424 beside U10** (BQ_TS 88.3 → 5.2 mm). | TS is a high-impedance analog input; 88 mm across the board picked up whatever it passed. | — | edit 41 |
| 6 | **R401/R402 22 Ω** (value only, still beside J2). | Espressif's hardware design guidelines recommend 22/33 Ω in series, close to the chip; their EV board fits 33 Ω. Moving them to U1 would reroute the USB pair, so only the value changes. | 0 Ω links | edit 42 |
| 7 | **Silkscreen labels** on the back at J3, J4, J5 and SW5–SW7, placed clear of pads and mask openings. | A board with no text invites a reversed battery or swapped speakers. | — | edit 43 |
| 8 | **Firmware R12: the in-spec DSI profile is the default** (560 Mbit/s, about 45 Hz); 1000 Mbit/s (Espressif's setting, 1.77 times the ILI9881C's 2-lane RGB565 limit) is `display fast`, for after a board shows clean patterns. | Bring up within the datasheet first, as the display review advised; then prove the faster profile on the board. | R11's single 1000 Mbit/s setting | `firmware/slim4` |
| 9 | **Firmware R12: 6 h of charge time per USB session** once a heat suspend has restarted the charger's timer. | Each exit from USB suspend restarts the BQ24074's safety timer, so repeated heat suspends gave it a new 4–6 h window each time. | — | `slim4_power.c` |
| 10 | **Rebuild from R26 by script**; new parts from KiCad's library, their board copies exported to `SLIM4.pretty` with timestamps stripped. | Two independent rebuilds give the same board, item for item, and byte-identical library files. | — | `R27_FROM_R26/build_r27.sh` |
