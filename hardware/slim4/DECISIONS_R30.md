# R30 decisions

R30 replaces PCB R24 with PCB R25 after the R24 deep audit (SLIM4_R24_DEEP_AUDIT), which found the DSI matching documented for R24 held only from the 0 Ω links to J1, asked for the impedance requirement per routed layer in the order, for the long thin supply branches at U1, for the board metadata, and for two firmware power-safety fixes; and after the owner's choice of the simplest battery: keep the 2-pin JST PH. Each decision names the file that carries it. `DECISIONS_R29.md` to `DECISIONS_R26.md` still hold for everything not listed here.

| # | Decision | Why | Supersedes | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Battery: keep the 2-pin JST PH and R412 1.8 k; specify a protected 1-cell pack of 1000 mAh or more** (503450 / 703450 fit the window). No thermistor port. | The simplest fix the owner chose. Charge is 0.49 A nominal, 0.55 A worst case: at most 0.55 C on 1000 mAh; a 1500 mAh cell finishes inside the 4–6 h default timer. The docs say plainly that the board does not sense cell temperature (TS fixed 10 k). | "any protected cell", "500 mAh and up" | `README_PCB_LAYER.md` *Battery*, order README, `PRODUCTION_GATES.md` |
| 2 | **Remove the 0 Ω DSI links R301–R306** and join each line directly. | They added two pads, a stub and up to two vias to every line and split each signal into two nets, which is how the R24 matching came to cover only the connector side. | R22's links | R25 edit 25 |
| 3 | **Match the six DSI lines in flight time from U1's pad to J1's pad** (423.9 ps each, per-layer model), not in length. | Outer layers and In3 carry signals at different speeds (about 5.9 and 6.9 ps/mm); the lines split differently between them, so equal lengths would leave up to 24 ps between P and N. Via barrels are left out of the model (at most about 16 ps between P and N). | R23's connector-side length matching | R25 edit 26 |
| 4 | **An In2 ground area under every In3 DSI run** rather than re-routing the In3 sections onto the outer layers. | On JLC06121H-3313 In3 sits 0.1164 mm under In2 and 0.35 mm over In4, so In2 is its nearer reference, and it was a patchwork of power pours with gaps. A ground band makes the In3 runs striplines between two grounds without moving any matched copper. | — | R25 edit 29 |
| 5 | **Order impedance control**: 50 Ω single-ended (100 Ω differential, loosely coupled), 0.152 mm on F.Cu/B.Cu, 0.135 mm on In3, stackup JLC06121H-3313. | The audit asked for the requirement per routed layer; the estimates on the published stackup give these widths. If JLCPCB's calculator asks for others, the routing is adjusted, not the fab's widths. | "impedance control not needed" | `CHECKS/build_builder_packs.py`, R25 edit 29 |
| 6 | **Keep 1000 Mbit/s a lane.** | Espressif's ESP32-P4 Function EV board drives its ILI9881C panel on two lanes at the same rate; the panel needs 624 Mbit/s. | — | `firmware/slim4` (unchanged) |
| 7 | **Reshape the In2 1V1_HP area** to the parts that need 1.1 V and feed it from L2 around the west side of the DSI band. | The 19 × 28 mm rectangle cut U1's and U3's 3V3 vias off the 3V3 plane (0.15 mm² islands): U1's 3V3 pins reached the plane only through 7.8–29.7 mm of 0.114–0.152 mm track. Now 1.0–7.8 mm. The longer 1V1 plane path costs some 10 mΩ (5–10 mV at 0.5 A), measured at bring-up. | R24's 1V1 rectangle; R29 decision 9 | R25 edit 28 |
| 8 | **Write the stackup, ENIG and revision into the board file.** | R24's Gerber job file said revision R20 and finish "None"; the fab sees the job file. | — | R25 edit 30 (`r25_meta.py`) |
| 9 | **Firmware R9: mute audio wherever the backlight cap applies; use only calibrated battery readings for power decisions.** | A 500 mA source has about 0.09 W left after the 3.3 V rail and the capped backlight, and no volume setting can be shown to fit; an uncalibrated ADC conversion can be off by more than the 3.3–3.6 V decision margins. | R8's unlimited audio and uncalibrated fallback | `firmware/slim4/RELEASE_NOTES_R9.md` |
