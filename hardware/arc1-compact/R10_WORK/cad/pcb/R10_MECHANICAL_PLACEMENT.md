This covers handoff rev 1.1 sections 4 and 5. The project is native KiCad 9: `cad/ARC1_R10.kicad_pro`, `.kicad_sch` and `.kicad_pcb`. The board is generated by `cad/pcb/gen_pcb.py` from `board/r10_board_plan.json`, the same source the schematic is generated from.

**Status:** first placement only. **The board is not routed.** The outline, connector positions, net classes, design rules and part grouping are ready for layout review. Nothing on this page is fabrication-ready.

## Case interface (to be confirmed by the enclosure design)

1. **Connector facets.** The connector faces are on the PCB flats, 4.9 mm inside the full Ø 119.6 circle. The case needs a flat facet about 48 mm wide at each flat, so the plugs seat fully; USB-A needs about 12 mm of mating depth from the face.
   - The alternative is to place the connectors on the circle with edge cut-outs, which puts the plug faces on a curved wall. Not recommended.
   - The top view stays circular apart from the two short facets.
2. **Outer diameter estimate:** about Ø 127 mm. That is the 119.6 mm board plus 1 mm clearance and 2.5 mm wall on each side; the circle grows or shrinks with the layout (handoff s1).
3. **Height budget (DERIVED, part datasheets):**
   - Tallest top-side parts: USB-A (≈ 7 mm), HDMI (≈ 6.5 mm), 4×4 mm inductors (≈ 3.5 mm).
   - Bottom side: 0603 maximum (≈ 0.9 mm).
   - Proposed stack: 1.6 mm PCB + 8 mm top air + 3 mm bottom standoff + 2 × 2 mm shells ≈ **17 mm overall**, before thermal checks.
4. **Insertion envelopes** (keep-outs on the case and the table surface):
   - USB-A: 22 × 12 mm (W × H) window region, at least 60 mm free in front, for thumb drives and receivers.
   - HDMI: a plug overmold up to about 22 × 12 mm.
   - USB-C: about 13 × 7 mm.
   - Each is noted on `User.Comments` at the connector.
5. **Thermal.** Heat sources: the 45F BGA and its 1.1 V buck, the D213 (≈ 0.53 W), the ADV7513 (≈ 0.26 W), the F133, and the regulators. Provide a conduction path (pad or plate on the top shell) above the 45F and D213 blocks, and vent slots in the lower shell only (no extra openings in the side wall: handoff s4). Board-level power is **OPEN** until the Lattice power numbers exist.
6. **Mounting.** Four M2.5 bosses at 0.78 R (≈ 46.6 mm from the centre) at 45°, 135°, 225° and 315°. Front USB-A support rib (see the J13 note).
7. **Service access.** All fixture pads (FEL, resets, D213 boot, D213 recovery USB, UARTs, both FPGA JTAG headers) are internal, reached by opening the case. There is no exterior service opening (handoff s4).

## What is still required before any fabrication output

The handoff's manufacturing package (Gerbers, drill, positions, fabrication drawing) is deliberately **not** generated. It would describe an unrouted board.

1. **Routing**, by a layout engineer or a supervised autorouter pass plus review. Critical items:
   - length-matched RGMII groups (50 mil, D21x HW guide 3.4);
   - 90 Ω USB pairs (10 mil match) and 100 Ω TMDS pairs;
   - the SDRAM group at 80 MHz;
   - BGA fan-out;
   - D213 0.35 mm QFN escape;
   - decoupling at the pins;
   - EPAD via fields.
2. **Plane design:** GND on In1/In4; power islands for 3V3, 1V2/1V5 (D213), 1V1 (each FPGA), 1V8 (HDMI, F133 DRAM), with return-path continuity under every high-speed group.
3. **Stack-up and impedances** from the chosen fabricator; then final net-class widths.
4. **DRC/ERC clean after routing;** fabricator DFM; assembly quote (handoff s4).
5. **Footprint verification** of every STD_LIB_CANDIDATE and GENERATED_DERIVED land pattern against the vendor drawing (ADV7513 EP, USB2514B EP, D213 QFN128).
