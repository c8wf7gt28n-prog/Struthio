# Graphite visual references

These are the five concept screens the owner supplied for the Visual Pass 1
revision. The direction is a late-night research terminal with the atmosphere
of *Serial Experiments Lain* and the visual economy of a 2600 hacker zine.

## Workshop stock, portrait layout

Use this as the primary worksheet composition. Let the source-bound result sit
directly above the large CRT portrait. Follow the visible amber path from the
selected rows through the menu cascade to the constructed chain. Keep the
character-cell bracket below the files. [Workshop portrait](workshop-stock-portrait.jpeg)

## Workshop stock, banner layout

Keep the selected rows, cascading commands, constructed chain, and preview in
one field of view. The user can see which rows will feed the operation, what
each step does, and where the derived sheet will go. The retained method has
its own place once it can be reused. [Workshop stock](workshop-stock.jpeg)

## Workshop stock, task progress layout

Make the current step and preview state visible without opening another menu.
Let the source record identify what the preview used. Keep the preview, save,
and later reuse visually distinct. [Workshop task progress](workshop-stock-task-progress.jpeg)

## Material comparison

Let the source and comparison sheet sit side by side. Follow the highlighted
value to its source passage, with its unit, conditions, and source location
visible. Give confirmed agreement and unresolved conditions separate lines.
Put the next question beside the evidence it depends on.
[Material comparison](material-comparison.jpeg)

## Overnight investigation

Give a continuing investigation a journal with what is known, what is still
open, and the next useful action. Keep sources, methods, checkpoints, and
outputs easy to find. The activity view should show the real worker state,
backend, and memory observations alongside the controls that act on them.
[Overnight investigation](overnight-investigation.jpeg)

## Carry the look into the native desk

- Use near-black surfaces, fine amber rules, cream reading text, and compact
  monospaced labels. Amber points to the active selection, action, or question.
  Green marks measured live readings and confirmed states. Give those states
  readable words or symbols as well.
- Use original black-and-cream halftone art in narrow headers, card corners,
  and quiet margins. Antennas, cables, and workshop machinery give the desk
  its late-night mood. Sparse ASCII fits the edges.
  Illustrated, ASCII, and Clean are the existing places to choose the ornament.
- Whenever panel art depicts people, use original female characters. Rei I
  from *Neon Genesis Evangelion* and Lain guide the quiet hacker atmosphere.
  Think watchful faces in monitor light, wired terminals, and radio gear.
- Keep navigation at left, actual work in the center, and evidence or activity
  at right. Use the shared Assistant, Terminal, and Log strip along the foot.
  The real head shows the full editable workspace path with Work, Programs,
  and System at its right.
- Treat the sample numbers, journal entries, and runtime badges in these
  concepts as examples. Native source, checkpoint, and activity indicators
  take their meaning from the actual records.
