## The three observed gaps

A real Qt probe reproduced three continuity problems in the preceding build: an explicit Settings destination could stay hidden by a previous filter, a focused Cabinet record could leave the left column showing Settings, and the Cabinet word could leave the Settings editor active in the center. NAVIGATION-01 connects those transitions through one small desktop controller.

The controller follows the current widget. Focusing Settings selects its current canonical setting key. Focusing a Cabinet record reveals its exact placement. Explore reveals only the checked context returned by its existing read operation; other workspaces retain the last cabinet anchor without declaring that the workspace belongs to it.

Following a view does not call its load_key or record-open form again. Settings values, draft revisions, cabinet writing and explicit read results remain with their existing widgets. A new Settings key or record still loads through its original route.

## Writing and the left column

Settings is now a retained Room, alongside Cabinet and Explore. Opening it from an edited source Preview no longer requires replacing that Preview. It does not consume any of the five pins or create a second Settings editor. Explicitly closing a workspace and its existing draft behavior retain their established controls. Settings drafts are still desktop-local; no restart recovery for them is added.

The Cabinet word returns to its retained record room without rereading over its form. If there is no retained record room, it opens the existing selected placement, or the cabinet root if that selection is unavailable. The Settings word retains its original shared editor route.

Explicit destination selection expands the required ancestor path and clears a filter only when it excludes that destination. Matching filters and unrelated branch folds remain. Resize handling keeps an expanded selected row in view; deliberately folded ancestors are not expanded by a resize. Selection and expansion continue using the existing validated Session presentation state. No navigation-history database or new preference is introduced.

The central record room now says FILED IN and shows its containing drawer path. The heading names the record itself. Show in cabinet refreshes the bounded cabinet projection and reveals the exact placement without reopening the form. The path follows placement parents; a second-project reference stays under that second project and continues resolving the same canonical record. It is not redirected to the owning home.

An unavailable or out-of-view placement clears the misleading current highlight and reports the bounded-view limit. It does not substitute another record or invent a filing relationship. Existing views and workspace search remain available. The controller reads existing metadata; source/master freshness and engineering applicability retain their explicit checks.
