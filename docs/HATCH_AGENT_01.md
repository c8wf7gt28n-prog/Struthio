## The loop (docs/AGENT_01.md)

`agent.assign` creates the assignment and its investigation; `agent.step` runs one planned step through the Kernel with a budget-bounded principal and records it before interpreting it; `agent.run` repeats within limits; `agent.pause`, `agent.resume`, `agent.stop` move the states READY, RUNNING, PAUSED, WAITING, DONE, STOPPED, FAILED; `agent.status` and `agent.list` read the five lines. Profiles are explicit Python; where one offers options, the model is asked in the new schema-constrained choice mode and its pick is taken only when the profile marks that option acceptable, every consultation recorded. The desktop driver is a timer that steps RUNNING assignments and yields between steps; Master Stop and closing Ledger pause them; the Today page offers to resume.

## The numbers on the page (the first task)

Hatch ▸ Work ▸ Documents, write a few quantities with units, then in the strip: "add up the numbers on this page". Slate reads the page, finds the quantities exactly where they are written (helper `quantities_from_text`, a fixed pattern and a spelling table, no evaluation), sums them with a dimension check through `math.evaluate` (exact arithmetic in the Kernel), appends "Total of the 3 quantities on this page: 16 kg · checked by Slate … · from: 12.5 kg, 3 kg, 0.5 kg" under the owner's words, and binds a condition card to those three spans. Units that differ become a QUESTION the owner sees and the assignment waits. Change a number on the page and the card reads STALE, the page shows a quiet line with the word recheck, the right column says needs you; recheck re-extracts, recomputes and rewrites only the result paragraph, bound again on the new revision.
