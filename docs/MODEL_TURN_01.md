# The model turn applied to a real page (HATCH AGENT-02 · WI-B)

November 6, 2026. Owner decision D19: Slate acts freely inside an assignment and asks only for originals, network and outside effects; **a document edit the model proposes applies at once, visibly, and can be undone**. Before this item a `documents.edit` the model proposed waited on a Run/Edit card like every other proposal. Now, when the page it names is open in the centre and the proposal fits that page, it lands there by itself, is washed pencil yellow for a moment, and Ctrl Z or the word “undo” takes it back. Every other proposed operation keeps the review words. Nothing here claims acceptance; the live turn with the 1.5B model is the integrator's (kernel level, build container).

## Where it lives

- `workbench/model_turn.py` (new, no Qt, no calls). `decide(action, page)` returns `{apply, arguments, reason, notes, corrections}`; `page` is `{document, revision, blocks, caret_block, selection}` plus, from the page, each paragraph's words (`texts`). It applies only a `documents.edit` whose id is the open document (filled when missing), whose `expected_revision` is the page's (filled when missing; a different one is **stale** and refused), whose change is one the store accepts (`bold`/`italic`/`underline` on or off, `clear` true, `style`, `replace`, `append` with `append_style`), on a paragraph the page has, with quoted words that are at that place. A missing selection is the owner's selected words, or the caret's paragraph for a paragraph change. The model's place stands whenever it can hold the words it quotes; the owner's selection takes its place only when it cannot (a paragraph the page does not have, or offsets where those words are not). Every correction that changes where the edit lands or who it is recorded as is also in `corrections`. `words(result, action, model_id, corrections=…)` is the sentence Slate says, and it ends with those corrections, so they are on the card. `keeps_places(change)` is true for marks, clear and style only (no words changed, no paragraph added). `request_words`, `changed_range`, `not_applied_words` and `model_short` are helpers.
- `workbench/document_qt.py`. `DocumentsTab.model_page()` gives decide its page; `apply_model_edit(arguments, request, model_id)` saves the page first, decides, writes `documents.edit` with the model's origin and the owner's request, applies the same change through the page's one undo stack as **one undo step** (`apply_locally` opens one outer edit block; the paragraph style goes first, then the marks/clear/replace, then an appended paragraph, and the edit blocks `set_style` and `append_locally` open nest inside it, so one Ctrl Z or “undo” takes a combined change back whole; the style goes first so a mark in the same change is laid over the heading font, not wiped by it), syncs exactly as `slate_request` does (reloading the saved revision only when the local application differs, and then saying Ctrl Z does not reach it), refreshes the condition line, and washes the changed range (`flash`): one `QTextEdit.ExtraSelection` in `AMBER` stepping down from alpha 255 over 1.5 s in ten steps; under Reduce motion it stays 1.5 s at full strength and then clears. The wash is never a character format, so nothing of it is saved. The caret is left after the changed words, so a selection does not cover the wash. `slate_context()` saves a dirty page first (the model binds its proposal to a saved revision) and ends with one line the model can copy, under 200 characters: `To change this page use documents.edit with id <id>, expected_revision <n>, selection {block, start, end, text} and change {bold|italic|underline|style|replace|append}.`
- `workbench/shell2.py`, `SlateFace.poll` and `proposal_card`, plus `apply_on_page` and `applied_card`. For each proposed action: a `documents.edit` with a Documents page current goes to `apply_model_edit`; applied, its card reads **APPLIED ON THE PAGE** with the program, Slate's sentence, the word **undo** (`tab.slate_request('undo')`) and *Was this right?* Right/Wrong (the existing `mark()`); not applied, it keeps Run/Edit with the reason above them. Other operations keep Run/Edit unchanged. The request recorded on the page is the owner's words (the job's message before the open-item context). Two edits in one proposal, both written for the revision the model saw: a later edit follows the earlier ones onto the revision they made **only while every earlier one kept the places** (`keeps_places`: marks, clear, style). Then each paragraph index and offset means exactly what it meant when the model wrote it. After a replace or an append the later edit stays made for the old revision: decide refuses it as stale, and its card keeps Run/Edit, gives the reason and adds that an earlier edit in the proposal changed the page's words. A later edit with no revision counts as written for the proposal's revision. A stale revision is refused, never rebased. The cards scroll into view.

## What the small model actually proposes, and what decide does with it

In the AGENT-01 live trial (build container, `build/agent01_ui/model_trial/`), Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct Q4_K_M proposed `documents.edit` with the right id, revision, offsets 4–15, the quoted words “quick brown” and `{bold: true}`, but paragraph index 4 instead of 0 and `origin: HUMAN`. decide uses the owner's selection only because that place cannot be right: paragraph 5 does not exist on the page, and the quoted words are exactly the owner's selected words. Slate's sentence and the card then say “the model named paragraph 5, words 4–15, which do not hold “quick brown”; your selection in paragraph 1 is used”. A place that does hold the quoted words is kept even when the owner selected the same words elsewhere: “make the fox in the first paragraph bold”, with the second paragraph's “fox” selected, bolds the first paragraph's fox. The store checks the quoted words again against the saved revision. A selection with no quoted words relies on its offsets, and it lands only on the revision it was written for (or one the same proposal reached by marks and styles alone). The model may never sign as the owner: its origin is always replaced by the model's, and Slate says “recorded as Slate’s model, not HUMAN”.

## The origin

Integrated (AGENT-02): `documents.EDIT_ORIGINS` adds `SLATE_MODEL` for `documents.edit` alone; `documents.create` and `documents.save` keep `HUMAN`, `SLATE`, `IMPORTED`. `model_turn.ORIGIN` reads it and is `SLATE_MODEL`, so a model-proposed edit is stored as `DOCUMENT_EDITED_BY_SLATE_MODEL` and `documents.edit` reads back `basis: MODEL_PROPOSAL_CHECKED_AND_APPLIED`, `model_used: true`; a direct rule edit still reads `EXPLICIT_RULES_NO_MODEL`, `model_used: false`. This changes one operation's schema (the origin enum of documents.edit), recorded in the registry digest.

## The instruction matrix

Integrated (AGENT-02): `instruction_matrix.APPLIED = ('documents.edit',)`. A `documents.edit` with origin `SLATE_MODEL` completes the latest `PROPOSED` row for the same operation and document id within the link window, `SUCCESS` with the reason “applied on the page” or `FAILED` with the error; an edit with any other origin records nothing, exactly as before. The owner's Right/Wrong marks on the card still win over the automatic outcome. No store was added. `tests_integrated/test_model_turn.py` StoreReadback proves the readback and the link.

## The fix pass after the independent review (November 6)

An independent reviewer reproduced three defects in the first implementation. All three are real, and all three are fixed here.
- **The chain (high).** The second documents.edit of one proposal was moved onto the revision the first one made whatever the first one did. After an append its paragraph index named another paragraph (a subheading landed on the added line). After a replace its offsets named other words (“own” made bold). It now follows only after marks, clear and style; otherwise it is stale and keeps Run/Edit (`shell2.SlateFace.apply_on_page`, `model_turn.keeps_places`).
- **The owner's selection over a right place (medium).** decide used the owner's selection whenever the quoted words equalled it, even when the model's place held those words, and the note saying so was never shown. Now the owner's selection is used only when the model's place cannot hold the quoted words. Every correction is in Slate's sentence and on the card (`model_turn.decide` `corrections`, `words(…, corrections=…)`).
- **Two undo steps (medium).** A change with append was two undo commands, so “undo” left the style or the replaced words saved as a new revision. It is now one edit block (`DocumentsTab.apply_locally`). The style also goes first, so `{bold, style}` applies in place and is undoable instead of reloading the saved revision.
- **The origin (low).** It needs the store, which this item does not own. It is still open (see The origin).

## The live page trial (integration, November 6)

The live page trial (docs/MODEL_TRIAL_02.md) ran Qwen2.5 1.5B and 0.5B through the real operations in the build container, and it found what the canned check could not. Under schema-constrained decoding both models filled every optional key of the change, so a "make it bold" proposal also replaced the words with nothing and added an empty paragraph, and the page applied it. The fix works at both ends. The model is offered one kind of change per proposal (one mark, a style, replace, append or clear, never an empty replacement). `decide` sets aside what rides along and applies one kind and one mark at a time. It replaces or deletes only the owner's own selected words, and a paragraph change applies only on the owner's paragraph; everything else waits for Run. An independent review measured the first version of the fix live and found four more holes, all closed. Page requests now also fit the owner's default 2,048-token context, which before failed every time. In the final run at that context, the 1.5B model applied bold, remove-bold and "replace lazy with sleepy" correctly, sent the heading request to Run, and changed no other word. The 0.5B model answers "bold" to almost everything, visibly and undoably. The rules still handle the common edits without any model. The rules live in `model_turn.tidy_change`, `kinds_of` and `decide`, and in `local_brain.page_edit_schema`. `tests_integrated/test_model_turn.py` holds the exact live proposals (LiveTrialShapes) and the review's cases (ReviewOfTheFix).
