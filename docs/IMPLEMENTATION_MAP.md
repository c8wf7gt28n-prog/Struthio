November 5, 2026 · UTC. Required by specification section 19: a short map tying each missing experience to the existing modules and the requirement IDs, kept current as passes land. Existing module names are starting points verified against the INTEGRATION-01 tree (commit fea2b5a); nothing here is a claim that the experience is finished. **Where Graphite 0.1 stands (November 6, 2026).** Graphite is the public name of the program (earlier Ledger & Slate); "Hatch" below is the menu the cat opens (`workbench/hatch_qt.py`), "Slate" the assistant, now Graphite, and "the Ledger Head" the head. Additive operations and settings are now verified against the recorded registry `docs/GRAPHITE_0.1_REGISTRY.json` (the release check); the evidence files of earlier passes named below and in the ledger left the tree at 0.1 and are in the project's git history and the owner's backups, and each named check writes its report again when run.

## Claim boundaries kept throughout

Operation counts and adapters are inventory, not finished applications (specification section 3). Offscreen captures and focused checks are mechanical evidence; the real model, real native handover, owner-host and physical columns of the implementation ledger stay open until their own evidence exists. No competing kernel, scheduler or data store is added; additive operations and settings are recorded in the registry record `docs/GRAPHITE_0.1_REGISTRY.json` (written by `tools/record_registry.py`) and verified by the release check, `tools/check_release.py`.
