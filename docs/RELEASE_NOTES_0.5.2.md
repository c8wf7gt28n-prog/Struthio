# What's new in Graphite 0.5.2

November 13, 2026. Guide → What's New opens this page. The 0.5.1, 0.5.0, 0.4.9, 0.4.8, 0.4.7, 0.4.6, 0.4.5, 0.4.4, 0.4.3,
0.4.2, 0.4.1, 0.4, 0.3.1 and 0.3 notes beside it describe what came before.

Three things the owner said on seeing 0.5.1, and one thing he opened for discussion.

## No keys are shown

"We don't need to display keyboard shortcuts anywhere ... keep it sleek and tidy."

Every key does what it did. None is printed beside the thing it does:

- **Menus** show their words: no column of keys at the right, no key written into a line, no underlined letter.
- **The Field**: the buttons at its foot say Do, Doubt, Trace, Check all, Brief, Look again, Where I was, Back (they
  said "D  do", "X  doubt" ...). The strip says "Whole desk · 35%". The rail's two buttons say Line and Run. A page
  read over the work has one Close. The line of keys under Stacy's steps is gone, and so is the "? keys" button.
- **Tips** on the bar above the work, the Find line, the head of the window and the marks of the writing page say what
  the control does and no longer end in its key. The writing page's first line no longer says "Ctrl Z".

One place still lists the keys, because it is the page whose whole purpose is to list them: the card of the Field's
keys, which the **?** key opens. Not gone over: tips deep in the older rooms (Engineering, Explore, the settings
editors) may still name a key.

## The glyphs in the menus are neon green

"I think the icons in the drop down menus could be neon green."

They are the desk's live green, each with a little of its own light under it (more as the glow is turned up), brighter
on the line the pointer is on, dim on a line that cannot be used.

## Neon is what the desk opens with

"I like neon setting." A workspace that has never chosen now opens on **Neon** (0.5.1 opened on Bright). Soft and
Bright are still under Rules ▸ Desk artwork ▸ Glow.

## Opened for discussion: Stacy with a free hand

"I want to discuss giving Stacy agency to control all of Graphite just like I can so I can tell her to do something
and an artifact is made just as if I had saved a file. I'm less concerned about her running out of control."

Nothing is built. What the builder proposed, for the owner to answer:

1. **The same hands.** Everything the owner can do becomes something she can name: every operation Graphite has and
   every command in the menus. She acts as herself: what she makes says "made by Stacy, at your word" and quotes it.
2. **Undo after, instead of Approve before.** Each act of hers is one line in a trail with one Undo.
3. **Her work lands where the owner's does**: Documents, the cabinet, and the Field as a new sheet wired to its sources.
4. **A dial**: Ask first (today) · Do and show · Free hand.
5. **A short list that still asks even on Free hand**, because it cannot be undone: emptying the Trash, anything
   leaving the computer, commands in the Terminal, power. The owner may strike any of them.

The limit that is not a matter of design: how well she understands plain words depends on the model running on the
ThinkPad. With no model she does what her grammar recognises; a small model will sometimes choose the wrong act, which
is why Undo matters more than Approve.

Asked of the owner: what should still ask on Free hand; whether she acts only when told or also when she notices
something; and which model she will run on the ThinkPad. The first slice proposed: the dial, and Do and show for the
changes that exist today, with the trail and Undo.

## What was checked, and what was not

The probes run the real window offscreen at 1920 by 1200 and 1366 by 768, as before, with the Field's buttons found by
what they do and not by the letters that are no longer drawn. Twenty-one tests cover the new parts, one of them that a
menu line keeps its key and does not print it. The project's own tests and the window gate were run on the exact
commit before this set was cut: see the set's START_HERE for the numbers.

Not seen: any of it on the ThinkPad's own panel. No second reader has gone over 0.4.7 to 0.5.2.
