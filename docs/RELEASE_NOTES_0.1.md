# What's new in Graphite 0.1

November 6, 2026. Guide → What's New opens this page.

**One name.** Graphite is the one public name of the workspace and its AI; the program was called Ledger & Slate before.
The window title reads Graphite 0.1, the assistant answers as Graphite (its pronoun is it), the menu is "the menu", and the
settings page shows two roots, Workspace and Assistant. Folders, scripts, settings keys and saved records keep their
earlier names, so nothing you made needs converting; pages that say "checked by Slate" keep working.

**The mark and the startup screen.** The sharpened graphite point sits at the left of the head; one click opens the STRUTHIO
game. Graphite starts with GRAPHITE on a graphite ground. The installers' shortcuts carry the same point.

**The cat.** An unlabeled cat at the lower left replaces the egg. On the desk it is pale paper on the graphite band, with
graphite eyes and paws and a small eraser-pink nose; its eyes turn pencil yellow when you point at it and while its list is
open. It opens the programs list (tooltip Open programs; Space or Enter on it too; one click even while it dozes): every
program and capability A to Z, one plain name and one small graphite icon each; type a name's first letters to jump. It rests,
glances sideways with a question-mark tail when you point at it, lifts its right paw while the list is open, and closes its
eyes after a quiet stretch. With Reduce motion on it changes pose without moving.

**The System menu.** The pale Graphite symbol at the upper right opens it, the programs list's mirror image: Game, Stop all
jobs, the process list (each row's niceness slider, the queue's order), Graphite services On · Off · Restart, then Log out,
Return to Debian or Exit Graphite, Restart and Shut down, each saying what it affects (this login session, this Graphite
session, this computer). Log out, Restart and Shut down save or set aside your work first, ask, and use the system's own
logind; where the system does not allow them they say so.

**A roomier centre.** On a 1366 by 768 screen at large type the centre keeps its width: the strip's quiet words give way
first, so Home no longer scrolls sideways, and no word in the window is clipped at any interface size the checks cover.

**The game at a desk.** STRUTHIO ARCADE 1.9.0 opens in desk mode: keyboard only, no title screen, a new run waiting paused
at tick zero. ← → flap, ↑ or Space flap hard, ↓ darts, Esc pauses.

**One release.** Graphite 0.1 is a single release without the earlier delivery records; the knowledge stays (the guides,
the decisions, the trial findings), and RECORD_OF_DIRECTIONS.md is the first page for anyone continuing the work. The
release check now compares the program with one recorded registry, `docs/GRAPHITE_0.1_REGISTRY.json`.

Not yet: the ThinkPad itself (its display, inputs and your model file) has not been tried; more assignments, native
application handover and model replacement with rollback come next. The daily guide (docs/DAILY_GUIDE.md) describes a
working day.
