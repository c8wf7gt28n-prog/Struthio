# R9 — power-safety fixes from the R24 deep audit

The SLIM4_R24_DEEP_AUDIT review of package R29 found two firmware gaps in the power policy. Both are fixed, and `sh tests/host/run.sh` now runs 42 cases (13 new); each new case fails with its fix reverted.

- **Audio had no limit on a 500 mA source.** The backlight cap left about 0.09 W of the 1.98 W a 500 mA port gives the charger, and the amplifiers could draw far more (two MAX98357A at 12 dB gain from SYS_RAW, about 21 mW idle, clipping into 4 Ω at high volume). No volume setting can be shown to fit, so whenever the backlight cap is on (USB below 1 A and no qualified cell, or a thermal charge suspend) the policy now mutes audio: the board holds both amplifiers in shutdown and drops queued sound. It lifts with the cap: a qualified cell, a 1.5 A / 3 A USB-C charger, or the battery alone. New in `slim4_power_state_t`: `audio_muted`; new board hook `slim4_board_audio_power_mute()`.
- **An uncalibrated battery reading drove power decisions.** Without the ADC calibration in eFuse the firmware converted raw counts with a straight 3300/4095 line and used the result for the 3.3 V switch-off, the 3.5 / 3.6 V cell checks and the battery-fault test, although its error can exceed those margins. Such a reading is now shown (`battery_approx` in `slim4_power_state_t`) but treated as no reading: no low-battery switch-off (the pack's protection board still cuts off), the USB cap and mute stay on, no charge suspend, no fault decision.
- Version 0.9.0. Built with ESP-IDF v6.1 for esp32p4 (no warnings); `tools/check_pinmap.py` passes on PCB R25 (19 GPIOs). Not yet run on hardware.
