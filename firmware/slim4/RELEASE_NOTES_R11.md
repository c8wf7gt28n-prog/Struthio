# R11 — hardware self-test for the first boards

The first SLIM4 boards come back from assembly with nothing yet known about them. From R11 the firmware checks each board for assembly faults at every boot and reports every check as PASS, FAIL or INFO, on the serial log and on a self-test page. `docs/FIRST_BOOT.md` (*Self-test*) says what each line means and what to look at.

- **GPIO nets, before any driver takes the pins** (`slim4_selftest.c`): every net with a pull resistor (15) is read with U1's own pull the same way, then against it, then driven the other way for 20 µs and released. This finds unsoldered U1 pads, missing resistors, a 100 k fitted where 10 k belongs, nets shorted to GND or 3V3, and stuck switches. PWR_WAKE's rise time through R110 and C601 is measured (0.2–3 ms accepted), which finds C601 missing or extra capacitance on the net.
- **Shorts:** 24 pins (the 19 board nets and the five unconnected U1 pads beside them) are driven high and low in turn at U1's weakest drive for 50 µs (1 ms on the two nets with 100 nF) while the rest are read, in two scans. A net that follows another is joined to it, and neighbouring U1 pads are named as a likely solder bridge. All 19 neighbouring pad pairs among them are covered. The charger's EN1/EN2 pins are never driven, and no net is driven against something holding it.
- **Chip and memory:** U1's revision (v3.x; v3.0 accepted, v3.1 preferred), 32 MiB PSRAM, U2's JEDEC ID (EF4020, W25Q512JV) and 64 MiB, and a brownout or power-glitch reset before this boot.
- **Panel probe:** before the ILI9881C driver starts, the firmware reads the panel's ID over DSI lane 0 with every wait limited to 20 ms (ILI9881C: 98 81 0C). A second exchange (ID again, then a write with acknowledge) is judged for error flags from the panel or the D-PHY.
- **Boot hang fixed:** the ILI9881C driver reads the ID itself, through an ESP-IDF read that waits without a time limit. ESP-IDF also asks for an acknowledge after every command, with the host's timeouts off. Through R10, a board whose panel did not answer (FPC not seated, an open D0 line, no panel supply) would have hung in that read and been reset by the task watchdog, again and again, with nothing on the screen or the log to say why. Now the display bring-up stops after the probe, the boot goes on, and the PANEL line says where it stopped.
- **Video and power:** frames per second from the DSI host. A USB host attached while charger PGOOD stays high is a FAIL (the VBUS path to U10). The USB-C advertisement, battery rail and die temperature are reported as INFO.
- **Self-test page** after the boot stamp: one row per check, then full-colour bars and 1-pixel line gratings. CLK and D1 carry video only, so this picture is their check. The page stays up 10 s, or until a control is pressed. The BOOT button (SW7) shows it again from the diagnostic screen, which now carries the counts in its header.
- `tools/check_pinmap.py` also checks the self-test's pin table against the board. Each GPIO must be on its pad and each net on that pad, and the driven unused pads must have no net. Inside the repository it also checks each pull resistor's value and rail and the two 100 nF capacitors.
- The boot log and screens say PCB R26 (they said R23).
- Partition table: the factory app (the recovery image) is named `factory`, not `recovery`. ESP-IDF v6 reserves "recovery" as a subtype name and warned about it on every full build. The update code finds the partition by subtype, so nothing else changed.
- Host tests: `tests/host/test_selftest_logic.c` runs the self-test's decisions on a simulated board, with solder bridges, open pads, missing and wrong parts, held switches, toggling status outputs, the panel probe outcomes, chip and memory identities, and power. That is 60 cases, 102 with the power policy's 42. Deliberately broken copies of the logic fail them.
- Version 0.10.0. ESP-IDF v6.1, esp32p4, full clean build with no warnings. Not yet run on hardware: the self-test's thresholds come from the datasheets (U1's 45 kΩ internal pulls, the parts' values) and are to be confirmed on the first boards.
